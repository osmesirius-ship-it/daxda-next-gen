### Extended Mathematical Definitions

#### Definition 1 (Density Operator Manifold and Bloch Representation)
Let $\mathcal{H}^d$ be a $d$-dimensional complex Hilbert space. The state space $\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H}^d)$ consists of positive semi-definite linear operators $\rho: \mathcal{H}^d \to \mathcal{H}^d$ with unit trace. In terms of the $d^2 - 1$ traceless Hermitian generators of $\mathfrak{su}(d)$ satisfying $\operatorname{Tr}(\Lambda_i \Lambda_j) = 2 \delta_{ij}$ and $[\Lambda_i, \Lambda_j] = 2i \sum_k f_{ijk} \Lambda_k$:
$$\rho = \frac{1}{d} I_d + \frac{1}{2} \sum_{k=1}^{d^2 - 1} r_k \Lambda_k$$
where $r_k = \operatorname{Tr}(\rho \Lambda_k) \in \mathbb{R}$. The purity is:
$$\gamma(\rho) = \operatorname{Tr}(\rho^2) = \frac{1}{d} + \frac{1}{2} \|\vec{r}\|_2^2$$
For a pure state $\rho = |\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, $\gamma(\rho) = 1$ and $\|\vec{r}\|_2 = \sqrt{\frac{2(d-1)}{d}}$. For the maximally mixed state $\rho = \frac{1}{d} I_d$, $\gamma(\rho) = 1/d$ and $\vec{r} = \mathbf{0}$.

#### Definition 2 (Lüders Projection Rule and Non-Commutative Joint Probability)
Let $A = \sum_{i=1}^{m} a_i P_i$ and $B = \sum_{j=1}^{n} b_j Q_j$ be two non-commuting psychometric observables with orthogonal projectors $P_i P_k = \delta_{ik} P_i$ and $Q_j Q_l = \delta_{jl} Q_j$.
If question $A$ is posed first and outcome $a_i$ is recorded, the state collapses via the Lüders rule:
$$\rho \mapsto \rho_{A=a_i} = \frac{P_i \rho P_i}{\operatorname{Tr}(P_i \rho)}$$
The sequential joint probability of obtaining $a_i$ followed by $b_j$ is:
$$P(A=a_i, B=b_j) = P(A=a_i) \cdot P(B=b_j \mid A=a_i) = \operatorname{Tr}(P_i \rho P_i) \cdot \frac{\operatorname{Tr}(Q_j P_i \rho P_i Q_j)}{\operatorname{Tr}(P_i \rho)} = \operatorname{Tr}\left(Q_j P_i \rho P_i Q_j\right)$$
Because $P_i$ and $Q_j$ do not commute ($[P_i, Q_j] \neq 0$):
$$P(A=a_i, B=b_j) \neq P(B=b_j, A=a_i) = \operatorname{Tr}\left(P_i Q_j \rho Q_j P_i\right)$$
The order difference is quantified by the interference cross-term:
$$\Delta P_{ij} = \operatorname{Tr}\left([Q_j, P_i] \rho P_i Q_j\right)$$

#### Definition 3 (Wigner-Yanase Skew Information)
Given a density operator $\rho$ and a self-adjoint conserved governance operator $K$ (such as an honesty/alignment measurement observable), the Wigner-Yanase skew information is defined as:
$$I(\rho, K) = -\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Tr}\left(\left[\sqrt{\rho}, K\right]^2\right) = \operatorname{Tr}\left(\rho K^2\right) - \operatorname{Tr}\left(\sqrt{\rho} K \sqrt{\rho} K\right)$$
*Properties*:
1. **Positivity**: $I(\rho, K) \ge 0$, with equality if and only if $\rho$ and $K$ commute ($[\rho, K] = 0$).
2. **Convexity**: $I(\sum_i p_i \rho_i, K) \le \sum_i p_i I(\rho_i, K)$.
3. **Upper Bound**: $I(\rho, K) \le \operatorname{Var}_\rho(K) = \operatorname{Tr}(\rho K^2) - (\operatorname{Tr}(\rho K))^2$, where the gap $\operatorname{Var}_\rho(K) - I(\rho, K)$ measures classical mixture uncertainty, whereas $I(\rho, K)$ measures purely quantum non-commutative superposition.
