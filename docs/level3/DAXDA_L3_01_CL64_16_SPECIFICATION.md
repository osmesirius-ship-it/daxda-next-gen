## 1. Mathematical Formalism

The Clifford algebra $\text{Cl}(64, 16)$ is generated by 80 orthogonal unit vectors $e_1, \dots, e_{80}$ satisfying:
$$e_i e_j + e_j e_i = 2 \eta_{ij} \mathbf{1}$$
where the metric tensor is:
$$\eta = \text{diag}(\underbrace{+1, \dots, +1}_{64}, \underbrace{-1, \dots, -1}_{16})$$

The dimension of the graded multivector space is $2^{80}$ discrete blades.

### 1.1 Sparse 128-Bit Blade Representation
A basis blade $e_I$ is indexed by an integer bitmask $I \in [0, 2^{80}-1]$, where the $k$-th bit indicates the presence of $e_{k+1}$.
Multiplication of two basis blades $e_I$ and $e_J$ evaluates to:
$$e_I e_J = (-1)^{\operatorname{inv}(I, J)} \prod_{k \in I \cap J} \eta_{kk} \, e_{I \Delta J}$$
where $\operatorname{inv}(I, J)$ is the number of bit transpositions required to order the generators canonically.

### 1.2 Involutions & Metric Norm
- **Reversion**: $\widetilde{e_I} = (-1)^{k(k-1)/2} e_I$ where $k = |I|$ (grade of the blade).
- **Grade Involution**: $\widehat{e_I} = (-1)^k e_I$.
- **Clifford Conjugation**: $\overline{e_I} = \widehat{\widetilde{e_I}} = (-1)^{k(k+1)/2} e_I$.
- **Scalar Norm**: $\|A\|^2 = \langle A \widetilde{A} \rangle_0$.

## 2. Implementation Architecture

Implemented in `daxda_engine/level3/cl64_16/`:
- `multivector.py`: Implements `Cl64_16Multivector` supporting addition, subtraction, geometric product, wedge product, left contraction, commutator brackets, and versor inversion.
