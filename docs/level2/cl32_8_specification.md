## 1. Algebraic Foundations of Cl(32,8)

$$e_i e_j + e_j e_i = 2 \eta_{ij} \mathbf{1}$$

where $\eta_{ij}$ is the diagonal metric tensor:
$$\eta = \text{diag}(\underbrace{+1, +1, \dots, +1}_{32}, \underbrace{-1, -1, \dots, -1}_{8})$$

### 1.1 Generator Classification
- **Space-Like Generators** ($i \in \{1, \dots, 32\}$):
  $$e_i^2 = +1$$
- **Time-Like Generators** ($j \in \{33, \dots, 40\}$):
  $$e_j^2 = -1$$
- **Orthogonality**:
  $$e_i e_j = -e_j e_i \quad (\forall i \neq j)$$
